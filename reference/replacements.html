Replacement queue

Every image extracted from the source decks that carries third-party copyright — a publisher line, a stock watermark, or a licence granted to someone else. This page scans every chapter live, so it always shows which of them the reader is actually using.

Most of these are line diagrams, and line diagrams are better hand-authored as SVG than regenerated as pictures: they come out cleaner, they recolour across the light, sepia and dark themes, and there is no licence attached to them afterwards. Reach for image generation only where the subject is genuinely photographic.

To clear an item: author the replacement, point the figure at it, and delete that entry from COURSE.replacements in assets/toc.js. The badge disappears with the entry.
