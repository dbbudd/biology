DO NOT PUBLISH ANY IMAGE IN THIS FOLDER.

Each one carries a visible copyright line, a stock-library watermark, a
publisher workbook page number, or no usable licence at all. They were
extracted from the Unit 3 PowerPoint and the Unit 3 anchor guide, where
classroom use is one thing; putting them on a website that students reach by
URL is another.

Replacements are tracked in the Unit 3 image review (../_review.md). Delete
this folder once every item has been replaced.
