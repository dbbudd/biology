UNIT 4 — COPYRIGHT-BLOCKED MEDIA
================================

These 26 files were extracted from `Bio U4 25-26 - Best.pptx` and must NOT be
published in the course reader. Each carries a publisher copyright line, a
named-artist credit, a permission grant made to a third party rather than to
us, or is an uncredited third-party photograph.

They are kept here only so a replacement can be authored against what the
original showed. Nothing in this folder may be referenced from a chapter.

Most of these are diagrams, not photographs. Hand-authored SVG is the right
replacement in almost every case — it recolours across all three themes and
costs less than image generation.
